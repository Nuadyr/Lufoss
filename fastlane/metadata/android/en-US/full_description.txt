Lufoss is a walking navigation app that just works. Search for a place, tap Start, and follow the map and the spoken directions. No account, no API keys, no ads and no tracking.

<b>Find your way</b>
• Search as you type, including exact house numbers
• Tap anywhere on the map to drop a pin and get directions
• Limit suggestions to your own country or search worldwide

<b>Round-trip walks</b>
• Pick 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 or 60 minutes and get a loop that starts and ends at your saved start point
• Tap Another route for a different loop

<b>Turn-by-turn walking</b>
• The map follows you, faces the way you are heading and zooms in for turns
• Spoken directions at three distances, which you can switch off at any time
• Automatic re-routing and arrival detection
• Preview mode lets you walk a route on screen without GPS

<b>Made to be comfortable</b>
• Smooth pan, pinch-zoom and rotate, with light and dark themes
• TalkBack, keyboard and reduced-motion support

<b>Private by design</b>
• No Google Play services needed
• Only your chosen start point is stored, on your device. Live location is never saved
• Map data, search and routes come from free OpenStreetMap-based services, and cached results are kept for one hour at most
